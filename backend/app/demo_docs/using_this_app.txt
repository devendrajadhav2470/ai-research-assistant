Using the AI Research Assistant

This demo collection lets you try the app without uploading your own files.

Suggested workflow
1. Ask a question about RAG, hybrid search, or how this assistant works.
2. Read the answer and check the citation cards for source documents.
3. Ask a follow-up to continue the conversation with prior context.
4. Create your own collection when you are ready to upload PDFs.

Collections
- A collection is a workspace with its own documents and chat threads.
- The Demo Collection is shared and read-only: you can chat, but not upload
  or delete its sample documents.
- Your personal collections are private to your account or guest session.

Tips for better answers
- Prefer specific questions over vague ones.
- Mention the topic or document theme when you know it.
- If the answer is incomplete, ask for more detail or missing aspects.
